# Evidence pack

Searched 2026-10-31: PubMed (2 queries, 14 + 4 hits), ClinicalTrials.gov (SGLT2i AND contrast nephropathy: 3 trials; Egypt filter: 0).

1. PMID: 39392560 · 2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis, 7 observational studies + 1 RCT, n=2,740 · Chronic SGLT2i use associated with lower CI-AKI risk in T2DM after CAG/PCI, RR 0.48 (95% CI 0.39-0.59); similar in elective and urgent procedures.
2. PMID: 38179307 · 2023 · Systematic review and meta-analysis, 4 observational studies (one RCT excluded for methodological concerns), n=2,572 · SGLT2i associated with lower CIN risk, RR 0.37 (0.24-0.58). Authors call the evidence inconclusive.
3. PMID: 38963097 · 2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis, RCTs and observational · SGLT2i associated with lower creatinine at 48 h and 72 h after PCI (MD -9.57 and -14.40 µmol/L), borderline significance.
4. PMID: 39890841 · 2025 · Double-blind RCT, Iran, n=121, PCI regardless of diabetes · Empagliflozin 10 mg from 1 day before to 2 days after PCI lowered cystatin C vs placebo; effects on eGFR mainly in patients over 60. Small, surrogate endpoints.
5. PMID: 42212353 · 2026 · Retrospective cohort, 2 hospitals, n=2,082 T2DM undergoing CAG/PCI · CI-AKI incidence 11.7%. SGLT2i use associated with HIGHER CI-AKI risk (adjusted OR 1.89; after propensity matching OR 2.38). Contradicts the meta-analyses.
6. PMID: 35795364 · 2022 · Propensity-matched retrospective cohort, single centre, China · Real-world CI-AKI risk in SGLT2i users vs non-users undergoing PCI.
7. PMID: 37276980 · 2023 · Multicentre registry (SGLT2-I AMI PROTECT), n=646 T2DM with AMI undergoing PCI · CI-AKI 5.4% in SGLT2i users vs 13.1% non-users (p=0.022).
8. PMID: 38858216 · 2024 · Retrospective cohort, n=366 T2DM PCI, China · Dapagliflozin users vs controls, CIAKI incidence compared.
9. PMID: 41751827 · 2026 · Nationwide nested case-control (Taiwan) plus cell and rat models · SGLT2i use associated with lower post-PCI dialysis risk (adjusted OR 0.33, 0.14-0.76); dapagliflozin reduced contrast injury in experimental models.
10. PMID: 42123715 · 2026 · Bayesian network meta-analysis, 67 RCTs, n=199,877 · AKI profiles of individual GLP-1RAs and SGLT2is (not contrast-specific); questions the assumption of class-wide renoprotection.
11. PMID: 38230484 · 2024 · Editorial · "Time for a PCI trial?" on SGLT2i and CI-AKI.
12. NCT04806633 · Greece · Dapagliflozin 5 mg vs placebo before catheterisation/PCI, planned n=1,722 · Status UNKNOWN (primary completion was due 2023-09); no results posted.
13. NCT06288529 · Turkey · Observational, n=975 diabetic and CKD patients · COMPLETED 2023.
14. NCT07231939 · Poland (FLORENCE) · Observational, planned n=4,000, PCI · RECRUITING.

## Gaps
- No adequately powered RCT with a clinical endpoint (KDIGO AKI, dialysis) in T2DM elective PCI.
- Observational evidence conflicts (items 1, 2, 7 vs item 5) and is prone to confounding by indication: SGLT2i users differ systematically from non-users.
- Effect of a short peri-procedural course (as opposed to chronic use) is untested beyond item 4.
- No registered trial in Egypt or Africa.
